Privacy policy

LinkedIn Job Outreach · Updated October 25, 2026

This is an independent extension and is not affiliated with LinkedIn.

What the extension reads

For job outreach, the extension reads the active LinkedIn job title, description, URL, and company link. It reads the company's displayed member count and the names, visible roles, and profile URLs of connectable people on that company's people page. For post outreach, it reads the active post URL and the names, visible roles, and profile URLs of people shown in its comments and reactions. Before sending a post invitation, it checks the recipient's profile name and visible role again. It does not read unrelated sites.

What you enter and what is stored

You enter your name, resume URL, job and post note templates, and filters. These settings are stored in Chrome's local extension storage on your device. A preview stays in the open side panel and is discarded when that panel closes or you change settings. After a send attempt, local history stores the recipient's name and profile URL, the job and company identifiers or post identifier, time, and result so the extension can avoid duplicate requests.

How data is used and shared

The extension uses this data only to show you a review list and to send the notes you select through your signed-in LinkedIn session. The default job note includes your resume URL and the job URL. The default post note mentions the recipient's comment or reaction. You can edit either template. The extension has no developer server, analytics, advertising, or AI service. It does not sell data or send it to the developer or unrelated third parties.

Your choices and deletion

Nothing is sent until you select reviewed recipients and press Send selected. You can delete the local sent history with Clear sent history in the side panel. You can change settings there. Removing the extension deletes its local extension storage. LinkedIn may retain invitations already sent; deleting local history does not retract them.

Chrome Web Store Limited Use

The extension uses data obtained through Chrome permissions only to provide its stated job and post outreach features. It does not use that data for advertising or unrelated purposes.

For privacy questions, use the support contact listed on this extension's Chrome Web Store page.
