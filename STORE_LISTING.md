# Chrome Web Store listing draft

## Name

LinkedIn Job Outreach

## Summary

Review LinkedIn job contacts or post engagers, then send connection requests only to the people you select.

## Description

LinkedIn Job Outreach helps you review potential contacts from a LinkedIn job or post before sending a connection request.

For a job, open its LinkedIn detail page and select Find contacts. The extension reads the job and company details, checks the company's displayed member count when available, and looks for connectable people on the company's people page. You can set job title, company size, and experience filters.

For a post, open its specific LinkedIn URL and select Find contacts. The extension lists people shown in the loaded comments and reactions. LinkedIn may display only some people, so the list can be incomplete.

In both flows, review each person's name, role, profile URL, and complete connection note. Select the recipients you approve, then press Send selected. The extension skips visible intern roles and profiles without a readable role or URL. It checks recipient details again before sending and keeps local history to help avoid repeat requests. You can edit the note templates and clear local history at any time.

The extension uses your existing LinkedIn session. Settings and sent history stay in Chrome's local extension storage. It has no developer server, analytics, advertising, or AI service.

Independent tool; not affiliated with LinkedIn.
